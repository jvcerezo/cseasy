## Full description (4,000 chars max)

Pass the Civil Service Exam with a reviewer you can trust.

CSEasy is an offline reviewer for the Career Service Examination (CSE-PPT), Professional and Sub-Professional levels. Every question comes with a full explanation, and every answer is checked before it reaches you.

WHY CSEASY
✔ Checked answers. Math and logic answers are computed, not typed by hand. Questions on laws quote the actual text of the Constitution or the law they test.
✔ Works offline. No signal, no problem. Review on the jeep, at work, or during brownouts.
✔ No ads. Ever. Not even in the free version.
✔ Learns what you miss. Questions you get wrong come back for review, and the ones you master step aside.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Verbal Ability (English and Filipino): vocabulary, grammar, sentence completion, error recognition, paragraph organization, reading comprehension, talasalitaan, wastong gamit, and sawikain
• Numerical Ability: percentages, ratios, work, age, interest, number series, and more
• Analytical Ability (Professional): word analogies, symbolic logic, assumptions and conclusions, syllogisms, ordering puzzles, and data interpretation
• Clerical Ability (Sub-Professional): spelling and alphabetical filing
• General Information: the 1987 Constitution, RA 6713 (Code of Conduct), peace and human rights, and environment laws

TIMED MOCK EXAMS
Take a full-length mock exam the same length as the real CSE: 150 scored items in 3 hours 10 minutes for Professional, or 145 in 2 hours 40 minutes for Sub-Professional. You can also take a 50-item mini mock when time is short. See your score by section, then review every mistake.

TRACK YOUR PROGRESS
Daily streaks, accuracy per topic, and a review queue keep you consistent until exam day.

FREE TO START
Practice the first 10 questions of every topic, take unlimited mini mocks and one full mock exam, all free. Unlock full access once for every question and unlimited full mock exams. It's a one-time payment, not a subscription.

FOUND A MISTAKE?
Tap "Report a problem" under any explanation. Every report is read and fixed.

CSEasy is an independent reviewer. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Civil Service Commission. It does not contain actual exam questions.
