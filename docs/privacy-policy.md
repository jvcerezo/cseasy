# CSEasy Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [DATE OF PUBLICATION]_

CSEasy ("the app") is an exam reviewer published by [YOUR NAME OR BUSINESS NAME] ("we"). This policy explains what information the app handles.

## Information that stays on your device
Your study data never leaves your device. This includes your answers, scores, review schedule, streaks, mock exam results, and chosen exam level. We do not run servers that receive it, and we cannot see it. If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted.

## Purchases
Purchases of full access go through Google Play. Google processes the payment, and we never receive your card or payment details. The app only receives confirmation from Google Play that the purchase happened, so it can unlock full access. Google's handling of payment information is covered by the [Google Payments Privacy Notice](https://payments.google.com/payments/apis-secure/get_legal_document?ldo=0&ldt=privacynotice).

## Advertising
The free version shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. Google may collect and use the following to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud:
- your device's advertising ID
- approximate location derived from your IP address
- information about how you interact with the ads
- basic device and app diagnostics

Where the law requires it, for example for users in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the app asks for your consent before ads are personalized. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings. To learn more, see [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). Users with full access see no ads, and the ads SDK is not started for them.

## Problem reports
If you use "Report a problem", the app opens your email app with a prefilled message containing the question ID, the reason you selected, any details you type, and the app and content versions. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself. We use reports only to fix questions, and we keep them only as long as needed to do so.

## Children
The app is meant for people preparing for the Civil Service Exam and is not directed at children under 13.

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the date above.
